Install

Set up in about two minutes.

No technical setup. If you can install an app, you can do this.

  1. 1

    Get the Claude desktop app

    Download Claude for Mac or Windows and sign in with your Claude account. Already have it? Skip to step 2.

    Download Claude Desktop
  2. 2

    Install Sheets MCP

    Download the file below, then double-click it in your Downloads folder. Claude Desktop opens and asks to install it. Click Install.

    Download Sheets MCP

    Double-clicking didn't work? In Claude Desktop, open Settings → Extensions → Advanced settings → Install Extension… and choose sheets-mcp.mcpb from your Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Ask Claude, then sign in with Google once

    In a new Claude chat, paste a link to one of your Google Sheets and ask for something. A Google sign-in page opens in your browser. Choose your account and allow access.

    Google shows a warning first, because Sheets MCP is an independent app that hasn't gone through Google's review process. Here's what to click:

    !
    Google hasn't verified this app
    Advanced1Back to safety
    Go to Sheets MCP (unsafe)2

    That's the only time you'll see it. Your sign-in stays on your computer, and Sheets MCP talks to Google directly from then on.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Sheets MCP is free. You use it inside the Claude desktop app with your existing Claude account.

Why does Google say the app isn't verified?

Google shows that warning for any app that hasn't gone through its review process. Sheets MCP only asks for access to your spreadsheets, plus your email address so it can tell your Google accounts apart. It can't see your other Drive files, your email or anything else.

Can you see my spreadsheets?

No. Sheets MCP runs on your computer and talks to Google directly. Your Google sign-in is stored only on your computer. What Claude reads becomes part of your Claude conversation, just like anything else you share with Claude.

My work account says “Access blocked.” What do I do?

Some companies only allow apps their IT team has approved. Ask your Google Workspace admin to allow Sheets MCP under Admin console → Security → API controls. Your personal Google account will work either way.

Can I use more than one Google account?

Yes. Ask Claude to “sign in to another Google account.” When you paste a sheet link, Sheets MCP automatically uses whichever of your accounts can open it.

Does it work on my phone or on claude.ai?

Not yet. Sheets MCP works in the Claude desktop app on Mac and Windows, and in Claude Code.

I don't see the Install option in Claude Desktop.

Make sure Claude Desktop is up to date. On Team and Enterprise plans, an admin may need to allow desktop extensions for your organization.

How do I remove it?

In Claude Desktop, open Settings → Extensions and remove Sheets MCP. To also revoke Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Sheets MCP.

Use Claude Code?

Install Node.js 18 or newer, then run this once in your terminal. The Google sign-in opens the first time Claude uses it.

claude mcp add --scope user google-sheets -- npx -y @sheetsmcp/server
