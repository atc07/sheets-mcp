Use cases

Connect your sheets to everything Claude can do.

Claude's sidebar in Google Sheets is great for working inside one sheet. Sheets MCP lets Claude bring in data from APIs, files and your other spreadsheets, and keep your sheets up to date without you.

Pull data from any API

In Claude Code, Claude can call an API, run a script or use another connector, then write the results straight into your sheet.

Build new sheets from your other sheets

Claude reads as many spreadsheets as you point it at, combines them, and builds a new one with the summary and charts.

Keep sheets up to date on a schedule

Ask once and a scheduled task in Claude Desktop refreshes your sheet every morning, without you watching. It runs on your computer, so it needs to be on.

Bring in files from your computer

CSV exports, Excel files and PDFs on your computer go straight into a sheet. Nothing to upload first.

Work across your Google accounts

Read from your work account and write to your personal one in the same request. Sign in to as many accounts as you like.

CLAUDE.md — ~/finance
# Finance sheets
- Cash flow: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Cx…
- Money columns: $#,##0.00, totals in bold
- New months go at the bottom of the Cash tab

## Month-end close
1. Pull Stripe payouts into Cash
2. Reconcile the bank CSVs in ~/Downloads
3. Update the Board Pack forecast

Teach it your way once

In Claude Code, keep your sheets and conventions in a project's CLAUDE.md or a skill, and repeat jobs like a month-end close come out the same every time.

Recently worked on

Every sheet you've worked on, one click away.

Sheets MCP keeps a list of the spreadsheets you've used with it, on your computer. Search it, pin the ones you use most, and tick a few to hand to Claude together, even across Google accounts.

How to open it

  • Claude DesktopStart talking about your sheets (“let's work on my budget”) and the list opens in the chat. Or ask “show my recent sheets.”
  • Claude CodeType /mcp__google-sheets__recent_sheets, or ask “which sheets have I used?”

The list has every sheet you've opened with Sheets MCP. Paste a sheet's link once and it's there from then on.

Install

Set up in about two minutes.

No technical setup. If you can install an app, you can do this. One install works in both Chat and Code in Claude Desktop.

You'll need

  • Claude Desktop for Mac or Windows
  • A Google account
  • About two minutes

  1. 1

    Get the Claude desktop app

    Download Claude for Mac or Windows and sign in with your Claude account. Already have it? Skip to step 2.

    Download Claude Desktop
  2. 2

    Install Sheets MCP

    Download the file below, then double-click it in your Downloads folder. Claude Desktop opens and asks to install it. Click Install (or Update if you have an older version; it can take a minute). It works in both the Chat and Code tabs.

    Download Sheets MCP 1.3.8

    Nothing happened when you opened the file? Quit and reopen Claude Desktop, then open it again. Or in Claude Desktop, open Settings → Extensions → Advanced settings → Install Extension… and choose sheets-mcp-1.3.8.mcpb from your Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Ask Claude, then sign in with Google once

    In a new Claude chat, paste a link to one of your Google Sheets and ask for something. A Google sign-in page opens in your browser. Choose your account and allow access.

    Google shows a warning first, because Sheets MCP is an independent app that hasn't gone through Google's review process. Here's what to click:

    Google hasn't verified this app
    Advanced1Back to safety
    Go to Sheets MCP (unsafe)2

    That's the only time you'll see it. Your sign-in stays on your computer, and Sheets MCP talks to Google directly from then on.

Privacy

Runs on your computer. Your data stays yours.

Sheets MCP is a small add-on for the Claude app. It talks to Google directly from your computer. There's no Sheets MCP server in the middle, and we never see your spreadsheets.

ClaudeDesktop app
Sheets MCPOn your computer
Google SheetsYour account

What Sheets MCP asks Google for, and why

  • Your Google SheetsTo read and edit the spreadsheets you ask Claude to work on, and to create new ones when you ask.
  • Your email addressTo tell your Google accounts apart when you sign in to more than one. It's stored only on your computer.

Nothing else: no other Drive files, no Gmail, no contacts. Sheets MCP's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Read exactly what's accessed and where it goes in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Sheets MCP is free and open source (MIT). In Claude Desktop it works on the free Claude plan; Claude Code itself needs a paid Claude plan or API access.

Why does Google say the app isn't verified?

Google shows that warning for any app that hasn't gone through its review process. Sheets MCP only asks for access to your spreadsheets, plus your email address so it can tell your Google accounts apart. It can't see your other Drive files, your email or anything else.

Can you see my spreadsheets?

No. Sheets MCP runs on your computer and talks to Google directly. Your Google sign-in is stored only on your computer. What Claude reads becomes part of your Claude conversation, just like anything else you share with Claude.

My work account says “Access blocked.” What do I do?

Some companies only allow apps their IT team has approved. Ask your Google Workspace admin to allow Sheets MCP under Admin console → Security → API controls. Your personal Google account will work either way.

Can I use more than one Google account?

Yes. Ask Claude to “sign in to another Google account.” When you paste a sheet link, Sheets MCP automatically uses whichever of your accounts can open it.

How do I see what Claude is doing in my sheet?

In Claude Desktop, Claude opens a live view of your sheet in the chat while it works. Each range it reads and each change it makes appears as it happens, and the view folds away when Claude is done. Click Open in Sheets to jump to the same cells in Google Sheets. If you've just updated Sheets MCP, quit and reopen Claude Desktop once so the new view loads.

Do I have to paste a link every time?

Only the first time. Sheets MCP remembers the spreadsheets you've used with it, in a list on your computer. Ask for a sheet by name, like “open my budget sheet,” or open the list: in Claude Desktop it appears when you start talking about your sheets, and in Claude Code you type /mcp__google-sheets__recent_sheets. It can't search the rest of your Google Drive, so for a sheet you haven't used with it yet, paste the link once.

Does it work on my phone or on claude.ai?

Not directly. Sheets MCP runs on your computer, so it works in the Claude desktop app on Mac and Windows (Chat and Code tabs) and in Claude Code. On claude.ai and the Claude mobile app, use Claude's built-in Google Sheets integration (paid plans).

One exception: if you follow a Claude Code session from your phone with Remote Control, Sheets MCP keeps working, because it's still running on your computer. The live sheet view won't appear on the phone (Remote Control relays the conversation, not the desktop's interactive panels); Claude shows each change as a small table in its replies instead.

How is this different from Claude's built-in Google Sheets integration?

Claude's built-in Google Sheets integration (paid plans) is the simplest way to edit the sheet in front of you. Sheets MCP is for work that reaches beyond one sheet: pulling in APIs and files, combining spreadsheets, scheduled refreshes and several Google accounts. It's free, runs on your computer and is open source. See the side-by-side comparison.

Claude stopped responding partway through a task.

Send another message and Claude will pick up where it left off. For long jobs with many changes, try the Code tab in Claude Desktop: it runs everything on your computer and uses the same extension, so there's nothing else to install.

I don't see the Install option in Claude Desktop.

Make sure Claude Desktop is up to date. On Team and Enterprise plans, an admin may need to allow desktop extensions for your organization.

How do I remove it?

In Claude Desktop, open Settings → Extensions and remove Sheets MCP. To also revoke Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Sheets MCP.

Use Claude Code in a terminal or IDE?

Install Node.js 18 or newer, then run this once in your terminal. (In Claude Desktop's Code tab, the extension above already covers you.) The Google sign-in opens the first time Claude uses it.

claude mcp add --scope user google-sheets -- npx -y @sheetsmcp/server
